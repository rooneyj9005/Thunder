What's in the pack

123 mods, sorted by what they let you do rather than what they're called. Here's the lot.

Automate Everything

If you've ever thought "I wish I didn't have to do this by hand," there's probably a machine for it.

  • Create: The heart of the pack. Build contraptions with cogs, shafts, conveyor belts, and mechanical presses. It's endlessly satisfying to watch your factory run.
  • Create addons: Enchanting via automation, confectionery, decorative blocks, and more, all designed to plug straight into your Create setups.
  • Mekanism: When Create isn't industrial enough. Ore quintupling, chemical processing, fusion reactors. Goes deep.
  • Applied Energistics 2: Digital storage. Dump everything into a network and auto-craft on demand. Never sort a chest again.
  • Refined Storage: Does a similar job to AE2 but with a different feel. Pick whichever clicks with you.
  • Thermal Expansion: Furnaces, dynamos, and machines for ore processing and energy generation. A solid middle ground between Create and Mekanism.
  • CC: Tweaked: Programmable computers and turtles. Write Lua scripts to control your base. Automate the automation.
  • Mystical Agriculture: Grow diamonds, iron, and other resources as crops. Farming, but for everything.
  • Storage Drawers: Simple bulk storage. Perfect for the 47,000 cobblestone you've accumulated.
  • Modular Routers: Move items around without pipes cluttering up the place.

Spells, Rituals & Witchcraft

Three completely different magic systems. Mix and match, or pick a favourite.

  • Ars Nouveau: Build your own spells by combining glyphs. Want a spell that launches you into the air, then slows your fall, then smites whatever you land on? You can do that.
  • Blood Magic: Power rituals with your own life force. It's dark, it's dramatic, and the payoff is massive. Build altars, craft soul gems, and bend the world to your will.
  • Hexerei: Classic witchcraft. Brew potions in a cauldron, fly on a broom, and keep a familiar by your side.
  • Apotheosis: Overhauls enchanting completely. Higher-tier enchantments, better loot tables, and boss spawners that actually challenge you.

Build Something Worth Looking At

Vanilla Minecraft gives you about six furniture options. Thunder gives you hundreds.

  • Macaw's everything: Doors, windows, bridges, roofs, fences, walls, stairs, trapdoors, and paintings. Every wood type, every colour. Your builds will look like actual buildings.
  • Chipped: A staggering number of block variants. Over a dozen styles for stone, wood, glass, and more. You'll spend half your time in the crafting station just browsing.
  • Rechiseled: Chisel blocks into connected textures and patterns for a clean, uniform look.
  • Immersive Paintings: Upload any image and display it as a painting in-game. Custom artwork on your walls.
  • Create Deco: Industrial-themed decorative blocks. Pipes, riveted metal, mesh panels. For when you want your base to look like a factory floor.

Explore New Worlds

The Overworld feels genuinely different from vanilla. It's worth going for a walk.

  • Biomes O' Plenty: Dozens of new biomes. Lavender fields, volcanic plains, bayous, cherry groves. The world feels far more varied.
  • Alex's Mobs: Over 80 new creatures scattered across every biome. Grizzly bears, hummingbirds, crocodiles, and things you'll wish you hadn't found in caves.
  • Oh The Trees You'll Grow: New tree species dotted throughout the world. Small thing, big impact on the landscape.
  • Nature's Compass: Looking for a specific biome? Craft a compass that points straight to it.

Cook Actual Meals

Steak gets boring. Cook something that actually gives you useful buffs.

  • Farmer's Delight: A full cooking system. New crops, cutting boards, cooking pots, and meals that give you speed, strength, or regeneration.
  • Create Confectionery: Chocolate, biscuits, and sweets, all made with Create machines. Because sometimes you just want cake.
  • Better Farming Plus: More crops and farming tools to round things out.

Quality of Life

The stuff that makes the game actually pleasant to play day to day.

  • Xaero's Minimap & World Map: A minimap in the corner, a full-screen world map when you need it. See where you are, where you've been, and where your mates are.
  • Waystones: Place a waystone, and you can teleport back to it from anywhere. No more twenty-minute walks home.
  • Simple Voice Chat: Proximity voice chat built into the game. Talk to whoever's nearby without alt-tabbing to Discord.
  • Sophisticated Backpacks: Craftable, upgradeable backpacks with auto-pickup. Your inventory stops being a problem.
  • JEI: Press R on any item to see how it's crafted. With 123 mods, you'll use this constantly.
  • Jade: Look at any block or entity and get a tooltip telling you what it is and what it does.
  • Lootr: Everyone gets their own loot from dungeon chests. No more racing your mates to grab the good stuff first.
  • Falling Trees: Chop the bottom block and the whole tree comes down. Floating treetops are a thing of the past.
  • Comforts: Sleeping bags that let you skip the night without resetting your spawn point.
  • Dynamic Torches: Torches you're holding actually light up the area around you. No more placing and picking up torches just to see where you're going.

Server Management

Running a server? These mods keep things fair, safe, and easy to manage.

  • SecurityCraft: Reinforced blocks, retinal scanners, laser tripwires, and security cameras. Protect your builds from unwanted visitors.
  • LuckPerms: A full permissions system. Set up ranks, control who can use what commands, and manage access across your entire server.
  • GriefLogger: Logs who placed or broke every block. If someone griefs, you can look up exactly what happened and who did it.
  • FTB Essentials: Teleport commands, homes, spawn points, and other server staples. The things every server needs but Minecraft doesn't include.
  • WorldEdit: A building power tool for operators. Select regions, copy, paste, fill, and replace blocks in bulk.

Already Optimised

You don't need OptiFine. Thunder already includes modern performance mods tuned for this pack.

  • Canary: General game optimisations across the board.
  • Krypton: Network optimisations for smoother multiplayer.
  • Memory Leak Fix: Stops the game from slowly eating all your RAM over long sessions.
  • FerriteCore: Reduces memory usage so the pack runs lighter, especially during long sessions.
